In [4]: research

Research

Multilingual NLP
Code-mixed text

Hate speech detection in code-mixed languages

People rarely write in one language online. They switch between Tamil and English, or blend languages the way Singlish and Konglish do, often inside a single sentence. My research looks at how transformer models can detect harmful meaning in that kind of text.

  • Transformer-based classification for code-mixed text in Tamil, Singlish and Konglish.
  • Language-specific adaptation, since one model rarely handles every mix equally well.
  • The core challenge: harmful meaning can hide in the switch between languages.

Experimental results will be added here once they're verified.

How I Think

A few notes I keep coming back to while building.

note 01

Understand the problem before choosing the model.

It's tempting to open with an architecture. I try to start with what actually needs to be solved, who it's for and what a wrong answer costs. The model choice gets easier after that.

// problem first, model second

note 02

Build, test, learn, repeat.

I'd rather run a small experiment than argue about how well something should work. Evaluation tells me where I'm wrong faster than assumptions do.

// the eval set is the source of truth

note 03

Make it useful, not just impressive.

ML, NLP and computer vision are only interesting to me when they help with a real problem. A demo that looks great but nobody uses hasn't solved anything.

// would someone open this twice?
